Session 3 — Open Mic: What’s on your mind? What are you doing in your courses? This session is a mix of an “open mic,” “community session,” and “outrageous opinion”-style session at SIGCOMM, but focused on topics of interest to the SIGCOMM education community. It’s a chance for anyone in the community to talk about what they’re working on or about a topic that they’re passionate about, to discuss ideas about what the community of SIGCOMM educators can do with and for each other, or perhaps to talk about “pet peeves.” Speakers in this session might address topics that came up during the morning sessions and warrant continued discussion. This session is intended to be an interactive and engaging workshop-style experience.
