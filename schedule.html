1:15–2:45 | Session 3 — Open Mic: What's on your mind? What are you doing in your courses?

This session is a mix of an “open mic,” “community session,” and “outrageous opinion”-style session at SIGCOMM, but focused on topics of interest to the SIGCOMM education community. It’s a chance for anyone in the community to talk about what they’re working on or about a topic that they’re passionate about, to discuss ideas about what the community of SIGCOMM educators can do with and for each other, or perhaps to talk about “pet peeves.” Speakers in this session might address topics that came up during the morning sessions and warrant continued discussion. This session is intended to be an interactive and engaging workshop-style experience.

3:15–4:45 | Session 4 — Birds of a Feather Breakouts (Virtual + In-Person)

Birds-of-a-Feather breakouts trade the whole-room format for small, self-selected groups, so the conversations that only got started earlier in the day have room to go deeper. Each group takes a single topic — a course design problem, a shared assignment or dataset, an assessment approach, a way to bring current research into the classroom — and spends the session on it, with the emphasis on concrete next steps the community can carry forward rather than open-ended discussion. Topics come from the community, so bring one you would want to spend an hour on.
